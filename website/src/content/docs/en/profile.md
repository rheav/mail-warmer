Some newsletter forms want more than an email: a first name, a country, sometimes a company or a job title. The **Profile** tab holds the details mailWarmer uses to fill those extra fields.

## A ready-made identity

The first time you install mailWarmer it generates a believable, fictional profile, so form signups work out of the box. You don't have to touch this tab. Change any field whenever you like.

The fields are: first name, last name, full name, age, date of birth (YYYY-MM-DD), gender, country, state / region, city, postal code, phone, company, job title and website.

## Buttons

- **Fill blanks** — fills only the empty fields with generated values. Whatever you typed stays.
- **Regenerate all** — replaces the whole profile with a new fictional identity. It asks you to confirm first.
- **Save profile** — at the bottom of the form. Click it after editing; changes you don't save are not used.

## When it is used

Only on form newsletters (tag <span class="pill blue">form</span>), and only for the fields a form actually asks for. Substack signups only need the email.

## Privacy

The profile lives in your browser's local extension storage. It is only ever typed into the signup forms of the newsletters you run, and never sent to us.
