## 2. What mailWarmer does

mailWarmer is a free, open-source browser extension that keeps email inboxes active by subscribing the addresses you provide to a curated list of newsletters. It:

- **Subscribes** — opens newsletter signup pages and fills them in with the email and profile details you choose
- **Curates** — comes with a hand-picked newsletter list that updates with the extension
- **Warms up** — optionally builds a natural-looking cookie profile before signups run
- **Records** — tracks the result of each signup so you can see what happened
