Mail Warmer - Keep Every Inbox Warm & Active

Mail Warmer is a free Chrome extension that keeps your email accounts alive
and valid. It signs your addresses up to a curated list of real newsletters,
so fresh or quiet inboxes keep receiving real mail and read as active,
trusted accounts.

Why it matters: a brand-new inbox that never gets any mail looks dormant.
Providers flag, limit, or even suspend dormant accounts — and they're far
more likely to block them when you sign up for social media, affiliate
networks, or new services. Steady incoming mail keeps the account looking
real and used, so it stays usable whenever you need it.

Perfect for anyone juggling new email accounts, social and affiliate
signups, side projects, or backup inboxes that go cold.

🎯 WHAT IT DOES:

  📨 Keeps Accounts Alive & Valid
     Subscribes your email addresses to popular newsletters so they keep
     getting real messages — so providers see a used account and don't
     flag, limit, or block it.

  📋 Curated Newsletter List
     Comes with a hand-picked list of quality newsletters, ready to go.
     The list updates on its own in the background — nothing to maintain.

  🙋 Fills Forms For You
     Opens each newsletter's signup page and fills it in automatically,
     using the email and optional profile details you choose.

  🍪 Cookie Warm-Up (Optional)
     Visits a few normal websites and accepts their cookie banners first,
     so your browser profile looks natural before any signups happen.

  📊 See Exactly What Happened
     Every signup is tracked per email, so you always know which
     newsletters worked and which didn't.

  🔒 Private By Design
     It never reads or sends from your inbox. Your emails and profile
     details never leave your browser. Free and open source.

✅ SIMPLE TO USE:
  1. Add your email addresses.
  2. Pick the newsletters you want (or use the full curated list).
  3. Hit Run — Mail Warmer does the rest.

No account. No setup. No cost.
